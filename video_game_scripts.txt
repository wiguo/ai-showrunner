# ENTROPY
### *An Interactive Career Simulator*
> *The Second Law says everything tends toward disorder. Nobody told you that included your career.*

---

## 1 · CONCEPT & FORMAT

**Logline.** A physics graduate who couldn't get into a PhD program takes one of two data jobs. The audience chooses the path — **Data Engineer** or **Data Scientist** — and lives through the very specific, very real pain of each. The twist: in every path, the *other* job is your antagonist.

**Format.** Branching interactive video (YouTube interactive / Eko / web player / TikTok "choose your fate" series). Viewers tap a choice; the player jumps to the matching node.

**Tone.** Dark workplace comedy with a melancholy underbelly. Think *Severance* lighting meets *The Office* deadpan, narrated like a nature documentary about an endangered species (the junior data hire).

**Runtime.** ~6–9 min per full playthrough. 3 decision points. 4 distinct endings + shared epilogue.

**Assumed POV.** Second person voiceover ("you") over a consistent on-screen avatar named **Sam**. This keeps the player inside the character while video-gen tools keep one face to track.

---

## 2 · STYLE BIBLE *(paste these into your video-gen tool's reference / keep them constant across every shot)*

**Visual style.** Semi-realistic cinematic, shallow depth of field, 35mm look, soft volumetric light. Muted **teal-and-amber** palette. Indie-film grain. Occasional diegetic **glitch/data-viz overlays** (floating SQL, YAML, scatter plots) that bleed into the real world when Sam dissociates.

**Aspect ratio.** 16:9 master; provide 9:16 reframes for the choice moments if cutting for vertical.

**Character — SAM CHEN (keep identical every shot):**
> *Sam Chen, 23, androgynous, slight build, tired but bright eyes behind round wire-frame glasses, slightly overgrown black hair. Wears a faded university physics-club hoodie over a wrinkled button-down — the "I tried to look professional and gave up halfway" uniform. Always holding or near a worn notebook filled with handwritten equations. Anxious, expressive posture; brightens when something *works*.*

**Recurring motifs (use as connective tissue):**
- A **chalkboard equation** that keeps getting half-erased and overwritten — first with SQL, then YAML, then a Slack notification.
- **Entropy** as visual language: tidy things drifting into mess. A neat desk by 9am, a disaster by 6pm.
- A **single green "pipeline healthy" dot** that the whole world depends on and nobody looks at.

**Choice-card typography.** Clean mono font (JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono), white on a 60%-dark vignette, two large tappable cards, soft glow on hover. Countdown ring (8 sec) so indecision *is* a decision.

**Music spine.** Lo-fi melancholic synth (reflection) · ticking tense percussion (incidents) · warm solo piano (small victories) · silence (the gut-punch beats).

## 5 · PRODUCTION & ASSEMBLY NOTES

**How to make it interactive (cheapest → richest):**
1. **YouTube + cards/end-screens** or **TikTok "reply with your choice" series** — lowest lift, post each node as a clip, link the next.
2. **Eko / Vimeo interactive / Mux** — true branching player; each NODE above maps to one video segment, each CHOICE to a decision overlay.
3. **Custom web player** (React + a small JSON state machine) — map the Branching Map in §3 directly to a `nodes`/`choices` graph; each node holds a video URL.

**Generation workflow tips:**
- **Lock the Style Bible (§2) as a reference image/prompt** in every single clip so Sam and the palette stay consistent — character drift is the #1 killer of AI video continuity.
- **Generate at the shot level** (each SHOT block = one 4–9s clip), then assemble in an editor. Don't try to generate whole scenes in one go.
- For the **glitch/data-viz overlays**, generate the clean live-action plate first, then composite SQL/YAML/plot overlays in post (After Effects / CapCut) — far more reliable than asking the model to render readable code.
- **Voiceover:** one calm narrator for the whole thing (the "nature documentary" register). Generate VO separately (ElevenLabs etc.) and time clips to it, not the reverse.
- **Choice cards** are static design templates with an 8s countdown — build once in After Effects/Figma, reuse for all three decision points.

**Continuity checklist per shot:** same Sam description · same glasses/hoodie · teal-amber palette · the green→red pipeline dot is the recurring "vital sign" — keep it visually consistent across both paths since it's the thread that ties DE and DS pain together.

## 6 · WHY THIS WORKS (design rationale)

- **The mirror structure is the whole hook.** In Path A the data scientist is the antagonist who makes impossible demands; in Path B the data engineer is the antagonist who blocks everything. Playing both paths makes the viewer realize *neither side is the villain — the system is.* That's the emotional payoff that makes people replay.
- **Each path's pain is real, not generic:** DE = invisible, on-call, judged by silence, technical debt. DS = SQL not neural nets, 80% data cleaning, hype vs. truth, models that die in notebooks. The specificity is what makes data people tag their friends.
- **The physics framing** (entropy → both jobs are "fighting disorder") turns a story about disappointment into one about transformation, which is what stops it from being merely cynical.
