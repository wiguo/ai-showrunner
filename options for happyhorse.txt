Useful options

- --resolution 720P|1080P (default 1080P)
- --ratio 16:9|9:16|1:1|... (default 16:9)
- --duration 3..15 seconds (default 5)
- --no-watermark to drop the HappyHorse watermark
- --seed <n> for reproducible-ish output
- --output myvideo.mp4 (default output.mp4)
- --print-url-only to just print the video URL without downloading
