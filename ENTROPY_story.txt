ENTROPY
An Interactive Career Simulator

------------------------------------------------------------

VISUAL THEME

Realistic, live-action cinematic - shot like a grounded indie drama, not
animation. Real actors, real offices: humming fluorescent lights, cluttered
desks, glowing monitors in dark rooms, the blue 2 AM laptop-glow on a tired
face. Handheld camera, natural lighting, shallow depth of field, a muted
teal-and-amber palette with a faint film grain. The only stylized touch:
subtle data overlays - floating SQL, a scatter plot, one green-or-red status
dot - that bleed faintly into frame when Sam zones out. Everything else stays
photoreal and lived-in. Think quiet, deadpan, true-to-life - the comedy comes
from how painfully real it all looks.

------------------------------------------------------------

SAM CHEN, 23. Slight, androgynous, tired-but-bright eyes behind round
wire-frame glasses, overgrown black hair. Faded physics-club hoodie over a
wrinkled button-down - someone who tried to look professional and gave up
halfway. A worn notebook of equations is never far. Anxious posture, but they
light up, helplessly, the instant something works. Remember that part.

------------------------------------------------------------

PROLOGUE

Sam didn't get into the PhD program. The rejection email came on a Tuesday and
misspelled their name. Four months of refreshing that inbox, ended by a form
letter and a typo.

Three weeks later, two companies want Sam. Both call the role "rockstar." Both
pay rent. Both say "data" and nothing else. Pick one - and know that in either
timeline, the OTHER job is the villain.

  -> DATA ENGINEER  - the one who gets paged.
  -> DATA SCIENTIST - the one who gets the credit.

============================================================
PATH A - THE DATA ENGINEER
============================================================

Sam meets The Pipeline: six years old, a billion rows a night, no docs, its
most critical job named after a coworker's dead labrador. Nobody knows what it
does; everyone's afraid to touch it. When it works, a green dot on the wall
stays green and no one looks. You are measured by silence.

2:11 AM. The phone explodes. The pipeline's down, the board report is due at 7,
and Sam is the only one awake. The cause, found in forty minutes: a data
scientist upstream changed a column from INT to STRING "to be safe" and told
no one.

  -> HOTFIX IT    - patch it, ship on time, sleep. It'll haunt you.
  -> FIX IT RIGHT - root cause, validation, late report, become hated but correct.

A-i - The Hotfix Hero.
Sam wraps it in a try/except so ugly it's brilliant. Red goes green at 3:41 AM.
The CFO presents flawless numbers and will never learn Sam's name. Six months
on, the patch has nine children, each commented "// TODO: fix properly." Sam
isn't an employee anymore. Sam is load-bearing infrastructure.

A-ii - The Quiet Professional.
Sam fixes the root, adds the check, sends the email no one reads. The report's
two hours late; a manager sighs theatrically. But it never silently breaks
again. Nobody throws a party for the disaster that didn't happen. Sam is
respected by three people, invisible to the rest, and asleep by eleven.

============================================================
PATH B - THE DATA SCIENTIST
============================================================

Day one at an AI-first company. Sam's eyes light up - neural networks, finally.
Sam's actual first task: a 4,000-line SQL query and a file called
FINAL_pls_use_this_one_v2.xlsx. The gender column contains M, F, male, 2, NULL,
and, for one defiant row, helicopter. Eighty percent of the job, it turns out,
is janitorial.

Then a VP who saw the prototype work once promises investors a live demo
Friday. It's Thursday midnight. Training accuracy: 99%. Real-world: a coin with
opinions. The model memorized the demo like a student who studied the wrong
exam.

  -> SHIP IT        - demo on data it's seen. Smile. Pray.
  -> TELL THE TRUTH - say it's not ready. Ruin Friday. Keep your soul.

B-i - The Demo God.
Sam clicks Predict. Flawless - because it's seen these exact rows, like a
magician planting his own volunteer. Applause. For one afternoon, Sam is a
genius. Then it ships to real users and the accuracy falls off a cliff in a
Slack channel with 47 people in it. A demo is a loan. The bill arrives Tuesday.

B-ii - The Honest One.
Sam says the three words a room hates: "It's not ready." Friday is ruined; Sam
is briefly the villain. Then Sam builds the real thing - smaller, slower, true -
and the one investor who matters leans in and nods. Trust compounds slower than
hype and lasts infinitely longer.

============================================================
EPILOGUE - "Conservation of Energy"
============================================================

Late, everyone gone, Sam opens the old physics notebook to a blank page. Sam
didn't become a physicist, and some nights that aches. But the physics never
left - messy data wants to become noise, pipelines want to fail, models want to
lie, and Sam still spends every day pushing one corner of the universe back
toward order.

Energy is never destroyed. It just changes form. So did Sam. The dream didn't
die - it just got a different job title. And, somehow, a pension plan.
